A curious mind.
A creator’s instinct.

Izaz AhmedCREATING WITH PURPOSE

I’m a Bangladeshi content creator and Junior Executive Marketer at Talent Synergy, with a love for stories that teach, engage, and stay with people.

I started in data entry at Staff Asia, where I learned the value of detail and a clear workflow. Today, I bring that same care to Canva video editing, AI-assisted content, course writing, and social media marketing.

Outside my day-to-day work, I create Bangla storytelling and educational videos on Who’s Izaz, and explore competitive gaming on Izaz Strike. I’m always learning, experimenting, and getting a little better with every piece.
